From selfie to profile photo

Three steps, no studio booking.

  1. Add a photo

    A well-lit photo facing the camera works best. Take one now or choose one you have.

  2. Make it professional

    Retouch gently, pick an office outfit, place yourself in an office and frame it for LinkedIn or your CV.

  3. Download in full quality

    Compare before and after, undo anything, then download at your photo's full resolution.
