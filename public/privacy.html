How we handle your photos

Plain-language summary. Template — have it reviewed for your jurisdiction before launch.

On your device

When you open a photo, it is decoded in your browser. Retouch, Light, Adjust, Crop, face finding and the standard background cut-out all run on your device; the photo is not uploaded for these tools. Downloads are created in your browser and carry no location data.

When you choose an AI tool

Outfit, Hair, Smile, HD cut-out and AI office backgrounds use an external AI service. Before the first upload in a visit, we ask for your agreement. Then:

  • A resized copy of the photo, or only the area being edited, is sent from your browser to our server and on to Replicate, Inc. (USA), which runs the AI model.
  • Our server holds the photo in memory only while the edit runs. It is not written to disk or logged, and unclaimed results are discarded after 10 minutes.
  • The copy uploaded to Replicate is deleted as soon as the edit finishes. Replicate automatically removes remaining prediction inputs and outputs about one hour after an API request.
  • AI office backgrounds are created from a text description only; no photo is sent.

Projects

“Save to Projects” keeps a copy in this browser's storage (IndexedDB) on this device only. Delete one photo in Projects, or everything under Profile ▸ Your data.

Enquiries and applications

If you send the contact form, your details, message and optional CV are delivered to the CAREer Studio team through Telegram, which stores them on its servers. A photo is attached only if you add one and tick the separate photo-sharing box. To have your submission deleted, contact us.

What we don't do

  • We don't use your photos to train AI models.
  • We don't build face templates or biometric profiles. Face landmarks are computed in your browser only to place edits and are discarded with the page.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. A short-lived, strictly necessary session cookie links your browser to AI edits in progress.

Your choices

  • Delete a photo in the editor (⋯ ▸ Delete this photo).
  • Withdraw AI consent or delete everything stored on this device under Profile ▸ Your data.
  • Only upload photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed.
